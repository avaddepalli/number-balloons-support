Privacy

Support privacy notice

This notice covers information submitted through the Number Balloons support website.

Information submitted

The support form asks for an adult’s email address, the support category, and a description of the issue. App version and device information are optional.

How information is used

Submitted information is used only to review the request, respond to the sender, troubleshoot app issues, improve Number Balloons, and prevent misuse of the support form.

Children’s information

The form is intended for parents and guardians. Please do not submit a child’s full name, photo, school, precise location, or other sensitive personal information.

Service providers

Form submissions are processed by the form service configured for this website and delivered to the support mailbox. Those providers may process the information only as needed to provide their services.

Retention

Support information is kept only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, maintain support records, prevent abuse, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Sale of information

Information submitted through this support form is not sold.

Questions or deletion requests

Submit a request through the support form and select “Privacy question.” Include the email address originally used so the request can be located.

Scope

This notice describes the support website only. The app’s complete privacy policy and App Store privacy disclosures must accurately describe the app’s own data practices.

Effective date: July 27, 2026
